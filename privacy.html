Privacy Policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

This policy explains, in plain language, what information the ACE Golf app ("ACE") collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

Who we are

ACE is made by ACE Golf Technologies LLC, based in Rockford, Illinois. Questions about your data? Email support@acegolftechnologies.com.

What we collect

  • Account info: your name, email, password (stored encrypted; we can't see it), handicap, home course and city. A phone number, profile photo, gender, birth year and country if you add them.
  • Your golf: the clubs and distances in your bag, the rounds, scores, putts and shots you log, your stats, and what Ace has learned about your game.
  • Location: your GPS position while you're in a round (for live distances and shot tracking), and when you use features that need it, like finding the nearest course.
  • Ace conversations: the questions you ask Ace and its answers.
  • What you share: posts, photos, comments, likes, messages, challenges, the friends you connect with, and your live round while you're playing (see "Who can see what").
  • Bookings and payments: tee times you book, and payment records (see Payments below).
  • Device info: a notification token for your phone if you allow notifications, so we can send them.
  • Reports and support: reports you file about other users or content, and anything you send us.

How we use it

  • To run ACE: your account, rounds, stats, green maps, Ace's caddy advice and round insights. Your shots and bag are what make Ace's advice specific to you.
  • To show your activity to the people you share it with, and to power friends, the feed, messages, leaderboards and challenges.
  • To book tee times and send the course what it needs for your reservation.
  • To keep ACE safe and secure: reviewing reports, preventing abuse and enforcing plan limits.
  • To send you notifications and updates about your account, bookings, friends and important changes to the app.
  • For marketing: news about new features, offers, partner courses and golf events, by email or notification. You can opt out anytime (see Your choices).
  • To understand how ACE is used and make it better. Anonymous, combined numbers (for example, how often golfers birdie a hole) may be shown to other golfers in round insights. These never identify you.

Who can see what

  • Other ACE golfers can see your name, photo, handicap, home course, city and bio. Your phone number, email, gender, birth year and country are never shown to other golfers.
  • Your posts are seen by the audience you pick: everyone, your friends, or golfers in your area.
  • Your friends can see your rounds on the friends leaderboard, your live round while you're playing (course, hole, score and pace), and the rounds you play in challenges you've joined.
  • Public leaderboards only show your rounds if you turn that on in Settings.
  • Messages are only visible to you and the friend you're messaging.

Who we share it with

  • Golf courses: when you book a tee time, the course gets your name, contact info, group and booking details.
  • Companies that help us run ACE: our app hosting and database provider, the AI provider that powers Ace (it processes your Ace questions and round facts to answer them), our payment providers, our email provider and our notification provider. They may only use your data to provide their service to us.
  • When the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users.
  • If ACE is ever sold or merged, your data would move with the company under this same policy.

We don't sell your personal information.

Payments

Card payments, like tee time booking fees, are handled by our payment processor, and in-app subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see or store your full card number. We keep a record of your payments: what you bought, the amount, the card type and its last 4 digits.

Your choices

  • Marketing: tap "unsubscribe" in any marketing email, or turn off News & Offers in Settings → Notifications.
  • Notifications: turn each kind on or off in Settings → Notifications, or turn them all off in your phone's settings.
  • Location: turn it off anytime in your phone's settings. Rounds still work, just without live distances.
  • Your profile: update it anytime in Edit Profile.
  • Blocking: block anyone to hide their posts, comments and messages from you.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete Account permanently deletes your account and the data tied to it.
  • A copy of your data: email support@acegolftechnologies.com.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your data, except records we're required to keep by law, like payment records for tax purposes. Anonymous, combined statistics that can't identify you may be kept.

Keeping it safe

Your data is sent encrypted and stored on secure servers, and each golfer can only access their own private information. No system is perfect, but we work to protect your data and will tell you if something goes wrong that affects you.

Kids

ACE isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has signed up, email us and we'll delete the account.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your data, we'll update this page and its date, and let you know in the app for any big changes.
